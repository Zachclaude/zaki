Privacy notice

Browser-only tools

The application plan is saved in this browser only. Use Clear plan to remove its schools and progress. It is not uploaded or synced. The HSK tool runs in the browser.

Email enquiries

The enquiry form prepares text locally and does not submit it to a server. You choose whether to send it using your email provider. The message then passes through the email services involved and is used to reply to your request. Do not send sensitive documents.

Hosting and external sites

The hosting provider may process connection and operational logs. External photos, fonts, official links and your email provider have their own privacy policies. No analytics or booking integration is configured in this release.

Your choices

You can browse without sending a message. Clear the plan on shared devices. For enquiries about messages you have sent, contact the address below.

Operator review

This notice describes the beta as implemented. The operator must review it when ownership, hosting or services change.
