Make a plan before you make an application.

Check what you can apply for

Pick a degree, teaching language and intake. Read the recorded requirements and follow the official notice to check your eligibility.

Explore admission summaries
02

Separate tuition from other costs

University cards show recorded annual tuition from selected degree programs, not the total cost of a year in China. Fees differ by course and intake.

Compare recorded tuition
03

Plan your everyday spending

Choose a city for a rough monthly range. The calculator excludes tuition; campus housing is optional and off by default.

Estimate living costs

A first-year budget needs more than tuition: add living expenses, accommodation, travel, insurance, visa costs and deposits. This site does not calculate an all-inclusive total or confirm a scholarship award.

Read these before shortlisting

Different universities ask for different things. These notices show why the course and application route matter.

01

Peking University

The 2026 written-test exemption route still includes a university review and interview. It is one application route, not a shortcut around admission requirements.

Read the application notes2026-10-16 ↗
02

Shandong University

At SDU, the general 2026 Chinese-medium requirement is HSK 5 for bachelor study and HSK 6 for master study, with separate Chinese-education exceptions. The course also determines the campus and fee.

Read the application notes2026-10-16 ↗
03

Shandong University of Science and Technology

Our SDUST fee and language records come from a 2022 brochure. They cannot establish the cost or eligibility of your next intake. Confirm a new notice before putting those numbers into your budget.

Read the application notes2026-10-15 ↗

Some official notices and detailed summaries are in English or Chinese. A recently checked source may still describe an intake that has ended.
