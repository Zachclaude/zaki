Funding

Scholarships to study in China

There are several kinds of scholarships for international students in China. They differ in who pays, what they cover and how you apply.

The main types

Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC)

Funded by the Chinese government and managed by the China Scholarship Council. Full or partial. Applied for on campuschina.org through Type A (home country) or Type B (Chinese university) channels.

How the CSC scholarship works →

Provincial and city scholarships

Local governments fund these for students at universities in their region. Some cover tuition only, some add accommodation or a living allowance. You usually apply through the university.

University scholarships

Each university sets its own, often based on academic results. Many are awarded together with your admission offer.

International Chinese Language Teachers Scholarship

For people studying Chinese language or Chinese language teaching, commonly known by its former name, the Confucius Institute Scholarship. Usually recommended by a Confucius Institute or other partner organization.
