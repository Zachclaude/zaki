Studying in Jinan

Plan your student life

What to check for this city

SDU’s International Chinese Education notice is program-specific; it is not the rule for every SDU course.

Examples refer to the stated source edition. Confirm a new notice before applying. These are researched planning notes, not a personal campus review.Source

Choose the actual campus

Ask for your program’s campus name and Chinese street address. Check the journey between that campus, housing and the arrival point before booking a room.

Ask these housing questions

Is a room available to your degree category? Is the price per person and per day, semester or year? Ask about utility bills, holiday access, deposits and cooking rules.

Your first-week checklist

Save the university’s arrival instructions, office contact and Chinese address offline. Confirm check-in hours, document originals and how to complete the required local registration with the university.

Food and daily routines

Ask which campus canteens you can use, opening hours and payment methods. For allergies, vegetarian or halal needs, request specific ingredient and kitchen information; a nearby venue is not proof of dietary suitability.
