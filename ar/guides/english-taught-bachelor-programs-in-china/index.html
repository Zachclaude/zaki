English-taught bachelor’s programs in China

Which subjects are commonly taught in English, how to find real English-taught programs, and what to check before you apply.

Updated 2026-10-14

An English admissions website does not prove that a course is taught in English. Start with a named program in the university's current international admissions catalogue.

Verify four separate things

Ask about the language of lectures, assessed assignments, laboratory or clinical work, and the final thesis. Check the campus as well: one university can offer different programs at different locations. For a bilingual label, ask how much teaching uses each language.

Use the official catalogue

BUCT's degree application page lists teaching languages by major. Qingdao's undergraduate notice separates its English-taught MBBS route from other courses. These are examples of why a school-level “English available” label needs a second check at program level.

Prepare the right evidence

Record the exact language score and accepted test version. If you studied in English, ask whether a medium-of-instruction letter is accepted and who must issue it. Do not assume the waiver applies across universities. Read the CSCA subject requirements separately; English teaching does not automatically establish an exemption.

Compare the full route

Check tuition, duration, professional accreditation where relevant, graduation requirements and any Chinese-language learning obligation. Before paying an application fee, save the dated catalogue and the admissions office's answers. This guide is a selection method, not a promise that a particular program is open for your intake.

  • Qingdao University undergraduate notice
