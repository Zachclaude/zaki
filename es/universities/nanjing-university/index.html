National programsFuente

  • Project 985

    Project 985 was a national program (from 1998) to build world-class universities. The term is still widely used as a mark of the top tier.

  • Project 211

    Project 211 was a national program (from 1995) supporting more than 100 key universities.

  • Double First-Class

    Double First-Class is the current national program (from 2017) for building world-class universities and disciplines. This university is on the second-round list published in 2022.

Chinese language programs

  • Long-term Chinese language training

    1-semester · 1-year — 10,500 CNY / semesterFuente

    Published standing guide: 10,500 CNY for one semester or 21,000 for one academic year. Listening/speaking, writing and HSK guidance are listed. Confirm eligibility, current application window and next start date directly with the college.
