Studying in China from Bangladesh

A practical guide for students from Bangladesh: applying, scholarships, documents, visa and life in China.

Updated 2026-10-15

This planning checklist is for applicants based in Bangladesh. It does not claim that every qualification issued in Bangladesh is automatically accepted by every Chinese university.

Start with your qualification

Send the chosen university the exact name of your qualification, awarding institution and expected graduation date. Ask whether it meets the course's education requirement and whether a provisional certificate can be used. Keep its written answer; a different school may decide differently.

Match your language evidence

Check the program's published teaching language and accepted certificates. If you studied in English, ask who must issue the medium-of-instruction letter and whether the particular program accepts it. For a Chinese-taught route, check the specific HSK requirement rather than treating the practice quiz as a certificate.

Build a home-currency budget

Record official costs in CNY first, then show an approximate BDT equivalent with a dated exchange rate. Separate tuition, housing, travel and one-off charges. Check the actual transfer costs with your payment provider and confirm university bank details through its official contact.

Locate the responsible visa channel

Use the Chinese Foreign Ministry's mission directory to find the relevant mission for your place of application. Check the current local procedure and document checklist directly. Residence elsewhere or a different passport can affect where you apply; do not assume a nationality-based blog post settles that question.

Keep two timelines

Track the university's intake and the funding body's deadline separately. Add time for final documents, tests and any requested certification. This is an organizer checklist, not a country-specific credential evaluation or a promise of admission.

Your country’s official starting point

The embassy’s December 2025 Type A notice directed applicants to campuschina.org and gave 10 January 2026 as its deadline (closed). Use this as a route reference, not a current application window; confirm a new dated notice directly.
