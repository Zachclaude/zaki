How to apply for the Chinese Government Scholarship

A step-by-step guide to the CSC scholarship: what it covers, Type A vs Type B, language and age rules, documents, and when to apply.

Updated 2026-10-14

Separate your university application from the funding route. The government overview distinguishes bilateral Type A and university Type B programs; the responsible call determines your eligibility and dates.

Find the responsible announcement

Start with the relevant embassy or dispatching body's call, or the university's scholarship notice. Record the edition, degree level, agency number, required systems and deadline. Do not copy another applicant's agency number or assume all countries use the same date.

Check the university requirements too

Funding eligibility does not replace the university's program requirements. Match your education, language evidence and required tests to the named course. Check whether you need a pre-admission letter or supervisor contact and follow the route's own instructions.

Prepare a document tracker

List each document, who issues it, its required language, whether translation or certification is needed and its validity. Submit consistent personal information in every system. Keep the final application, receipts where applicable and official correspondence.

Keep a backup plan

Distinguish submitted, recommended and awarded statuses. A school recommendation does not justify treating the scholarship as confirmed. Ask what the award covers, when it starts and which conditions apply. If you cannot afford self-funded study, do not make non-refundable commitments before receiving confirmed funding and the necessary admission instructions.
