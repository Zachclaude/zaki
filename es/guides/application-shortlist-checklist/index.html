Guías

Build a shortlist you can actually apply to

A practical worksheet for comparing Chinese universities before paying an application fee.

Updated 2026-10-14

Start with the course you want, its teaching language and a budget you can maintain. A ranking is useful context; it is not an admission requirement.

Build one row per program

Record the university, campus, degree, official brochure, intake year, education requirement, language threshold, required tests, tuition, housing and deadline. Use “not confirmed” for a missing value, rather than zero. Different programs at the same university can have different rules.

Check before paying

Read the dated notice. Separate self-funded and scholarship deadlines. Check whether you can submit a provisional graduation certificate, which CSCA subjects your undergraduate major requires, and whether an English-medium certificate is accepted instead of a test score. Ask the university if the brochure does not answer your question.

Keep a decision log

Save the official link, the version you read and the date of the school's email reply. Label each choice: eligible on published requirements, needs clarification, or does not meet a stated condition. These labels are planning notes, not predictions of admission.

Email you can adapt

“I am considering [program] for [intake]. My current qualification is [qualification] and my language evidence is [certificate]. Could you confirm [one specific missing requirement] and the official application link?”

Send documents to the university only through its confirmed channel. Do not upload passports or transcripts to Zaki. We help you organize your questions; the institution decides your application.
