Scholarship guide

The Chinese Government Scholarship, explained

The Chinese Government Scholarship, often called the CSC scholarship, is funded by the Chinese government and managed by the China Scholarship Council. It's the best-known scholarship for studying in China, and the most competitive.

The only official application system

You apply online at campuschina.org. No agent, website or person can apply for you through a secret channel, and the scholarship itself is free to apply for.

Go to campuschina.org ↗

Type A or Type B?

A

Type A – through your home country

You apply through your home country's dispatching authority (often a ministry of education or scholarship office) or the Chinese embassy or consulate. They nominate candidates to the China Scholarship Council.

B

Type B – through a Chinese university

You apply directly to a Chinese university that is authorized to accept Chinese Government Scholarship students. The university reviews applications and recommends students.

Your country and the university decide which channels are open to you. You can find the agencies and universities on campuschina.org.

What it covers

A full scholarship normally covers tuition, on-campus accommodation, a monthly living allowance and comprehensive medical insurance. Partial scholarships cover one or more of these. The exact amounts are set in each year's official call.

Rules people often miss

  • You can't hold the Chinese Government Scholarship together with any other scholarship funded by the Chinese government.
  • Undergraduate applicants need CSCA results. See our CSCA page for details.
  • Each channel has its own deadline, and deadlines differ by country and by university.
  • Age limits and degree requirements apply. Check the current call before you apply.

Be realistic

Most international students in China are self-funded, and the scholarship is highly competitive. Apply, but also look at self-funded options, provincial and university scholarships so you have a plan B.
