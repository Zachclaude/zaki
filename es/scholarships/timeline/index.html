Plan ahead

The study in China application timeline

Most degree programs in China start in September, and a few also take students in March. Here is the usual pattern for a September start. Your country's embassy and each university set their own dates, so use this to plan, then confirm the real deadlines.

Typical pattern – confirm your country's dates.

  1. 1
    Sep – Nov

    Choose universities, prepare documents, take language tests and the CSCA

    • Shortlist three to six programs and read each one's requirements.
    • Book language tests (IELTS, TOEFL or HSK) and, for undergraduate applicants, check whether the CSCA is required.
    • Start collecting documents that take time: notarized diplomas, transcripts, a non-criminal record and recommendation letters.
  2. 2
    Dec

    Chinese Government Scholarship system opens, CSCA exam period

    • The Chinese Government Scholarship application system on campuschina.org usually opens around December.
    • Type B applicants contact their chosen universities; Type A applicants check with their home country's dispatching authority or the Chinese embassy.
  3. 3
    Jan – Apr

    Embassy and university deadlines

    • Embassy (Type A) and university (Type B) deadlines usually fall in this window, and many are earlier than you'd expect.
    • Self-funded applications often stay open longer, but popular programs can fill up.
  4. 4
    May – Jul

    Admission and scholarship results

    • Universities send admission decisions, and scholarship results follow.
    • Reply to offers on time and keep every email from the university.
  5. 5
    Jul – Aug

    Admission letter and JW201 / JW202, apply for an X1 or X2 visa

    • Your admission letter and JW201 (scholarship) or JW202 form arrive by post or as an official electronic copy.
    • Apply for an X1 visa for study over 180 days, or X2 for 180 days or less, at the Chinese embassy or visa center.
  6. 6
    Sep

    Arrive, register and enroll

    • Register on campus and, if you hold an X1 visa, apply for your residence permit within 30 days of arrival.
    • Complete the physical examination check if your university asks for it.

Starting in March? Shift everything about six months earlier and check whether your program offers a spring intake at all.
