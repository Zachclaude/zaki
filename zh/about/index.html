About Zaki

Zaki 背后是谁？

我是 Zach，一名中国大学生，有申请中国大学的经历。我发起 Zaki，希望帮助打算来华留学的同学理清分散的信息。

我负责网站方向，并分享自己的申请经验；AI 工具辅助网站开发、翻译和资料整理。我的个人经验不能代替各校针对国际学生发布的招生要求。

Why this site exists

Information about studying in China is scattered across university websites, embassy notices and social media posts. A lot of it is out of date, and some of it comes from people who want your money.

I bring the useful parts together, link every fact to its official source, and explain it the way I would to a friend.

What we are not

We are not a university, a government office or an admissions agency. We can't admit you, award a scholarship or issue a visa. Only universities, scholarship bodies and embassies can.

How we keep information accurate

University and scholarship data is entered by hand from official websites. Each record shows the source link and the date we last checked it. Records that haven't been checked for a long time are removed until someone verifies them again.

If you find a mistake, email me and I'll fix it.

How we make money

Some pages contain affiliate links, and we offer paid consultations and guides. See our disclosure page for details.
