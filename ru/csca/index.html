Admission test

The CSCA test for undergraduate applicants

What the CSCA (China Scholastic Competency Assessment) is, who needs it, its subjects, how often it runs, what it costs and how results work – with a link to the official source for each point.

Updated 2026-10-15

The CSCA (China Scholastic Competency Assessment, 来华留学本科入学学业水平测试) is a standard academic test for international students applying to bachelor's programs at Chinese universities. It started in December 2025. Universities use the results alongside your school grades, the way universities in other countries use SAT or A-level results.

Requirements are set by each university and program. Before you register, check which CSCA subjects your target program asks for, then register only on csca.cn.

Who needs to take it

The CSCA is for undergraduate (bachelor's) applicants. Chinese Government Scholarship applicants at bachelor's level were the first group required to take it, and more universities are asking all bachelor's applicants for results. Your university's admission notice tells you whether you need it.

Do not confuse exemption from the Chinese subject with exemption from the whole test. An HSK certificate, a Chinese-language major or a foundation course does not by itself establish a full exemption. Ask your target university to confirm the exact subjects and any exemption in writing. Official registration and notices.

Do not assume that A-levels, IB, AP, SAT or ACT replace a requested CSCA score. Follow the current university admission notice and confirm any alternative directly with admissions.

Subjects

  • Chinese for academic purposes (专业中文) – two versions: one for humanities and social sciences, one for science, engineering and medicine. Answered in Chinese.
  • Mathematics – required for almost every program.
  • Physics and Chemistry – each university decides which programs need them. Science, engineering, agriculture and medicine programs usually ask for at least one of the two.

You can take mathematics, physics and chemistry in Chinese or English. Choose the language that matches your program, or the one your university asks for.

Applying to an English-taught program?

Applicants to English-taught bachelor's programs normally don't take the Chinese test, but still take mathematics and any physics or chemistry paper the program requires. Check each program, because some universities set extra conditions.

How often it runs

Use the current official CSCA announcements for session dates, registration deadlines, available locations and test formats. A calendar from an earlier admission year is not a booking schedule for the next year. Check that your results will arrive before your university's document deadline.

Fees

The BLCU instructions published in December 2025 list 450 CNY for one subject and 700 CNY for two or more subjects. These are historical published figures, not a confirmed price for every later session. Check the amount in the current official registration flow before paying.

Results

Check the official session notice and score instructions for result timing, report access and validity. We do not promise a fixed turnaround. Check the university's submission instructions as well: a request to upload a score report is different from automatic score delivery.

How to register

  1. Confirm the subjects your target programs require.
  2. Create an account on csca.cn and register for a session.
  3. Pay the fee in the CSCA system, never to a person or an agent.
  4. Prepare with the official syllabus and practice tests on csca.cn.
