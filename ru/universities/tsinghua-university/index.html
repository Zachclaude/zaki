Бакалавриат · 2027 intake

Проверено 2026-10-13

Версия источника Дата проверки не означает, что приём открыт. Уточните программу, набор и срок на официальном сайте.

На китайском
Chinese-English taught programs: HSK 5 or above with over 60 in listening, reading and writing, plus strong English (TOEFL, IELTS or similar)
На английском
Official English test result (TOEFL, IELTS or similar) if English isn't your first language; no Chinese test needed
Образование
High school graduation certificate from full-time, in-person study, with outstanding grades
Возраст
At least 18 on 1 September 2027 (under-18s need a guardian in China)
CSCA
Accepted as supporting evidence alongside national exams, SAT, ACT, A-Level, AP or IB results
Срок подачи
Round 1: 30 September – 20 November 2026 (results by January 2027). Round 2: 21 November 2026 – 28 February 2027 (results early May 2027)
Сбор за заявку
800 CNY
Стоимость обучения
26,000–100,000 CNY / year – 26,000 for economics, law and some colleges; 30,000 for science and engineering; 40,000 for arts; 100,000 for English-taught programs
Другие требования
  • Comprehensive assessment after document screening
  • Portfolio for design, fine arts and architecture

National programsИсточник

  • Project 985

    Project 985 was a national program (from 1998) to build world-class universities. The term is still widely used as a mark of the top tier.

  • Project 211

    Project 211 was a national program (from 1995) supporting more than 100 key universities.

  • Double First-Class

    Double First-Class is the current national program (from 2017) for building world-class universities and disciplines. This university is on the second-round list published in 2022.
