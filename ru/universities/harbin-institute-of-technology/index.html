Бакалавриат · 2026 intake — closed

Проверено 2026-10-16

Версия источника Дата проверки не означает, что приём открыт. Уточните программу, набор и срок на официальном сайте.

На китайском
HSK 4 ≥210, valid 2 years, except Chinese Language and Literature; previous Chinese-medium certificate can substitute.
На английском
Page states Academic IELTS >6.0 (each ≥5.5) or TOEFL iBT >80; recognized alternatives/English-medium evidence accepted. Confirm borderline scores directly.
Образование
High-school diploma; expected-graduation evidence accepted initially.
Возраст
Under 30; HIT Scholarship under 25.
CSCA
Required; subject list attached to the official page.
Срок подачи
September 2026 deadline: 15 July 2026 (closed); no 2027 deadline inferred.
Сбор за заявку
400 CNY
Стоимость обучения
20,000–26,000 CNY / year – Chinese 20,000; English 26,000 CNY/year. HIT Scholarship application fee is 600 rather than the self-funded 400.
Другие требования
  • English-medium classes need at least 20 students; availability is not assured.

Магистратура · 2026 intake — closed

Проверено 2026-10-16

Версия источника Дата проверки не означает, что приём открыт. Уточните программу, набор и срок на официальном сайте.

На китайском
HSK 4 ≥210, valid 2 years; previous Chinese-medium degree certificate accepted.
На английском
TOEFL ≥80 / Academic IELTS ≥6.0, each ≥5.5, or approved equivalent/English-medium degree certificate.
Образование
Bachelor's degree; expected-graduation certificate initially accepted.
Возраст
Under 35; HIT Scholarship under 30.
CSCA
Не записано
Срок подачи
September 2026 deadline: 31 May 2026 (closed). CSC B stages had earlier deadlines; do not reuse for 2027.
Сбор за заявку
400 CNY
Стоимость обучения
28,000–34,000 CNY / year – Chinese 28,000; English 34,000 CNY/year; HIT Scholarship application fee 600.
Другие требования
  • ≥1,500-word study plan; two academic references; deposit evidence ≥30,000 CNY.
  • Supervisor acceptance encouraged, not mandatory under this guide.

National programsИсточник

  • Project 985

    Project 985 was a national program (from 1998) to build world-class universities. The term is still widely used as a mark of the top tier.

  • Project 211

    Project 211 was a national program (from 1995) supporting more than 100 key universities.

  • Double First-Class

    Double First-Class is the current national program (from 2017) for building world-class universities and disciplines. This university is on the second-round list published in 2022.
