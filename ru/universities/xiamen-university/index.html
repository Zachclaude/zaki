Версия источника Дата проверки не означает, что приём открыт. Уточните программу, набор и срок на официальном сайте.

На китайском
HSK 5 ≥210 for humanities/economics/management/law/arts; HSK 4 ≥210 for science/engineering/medicine/PE. Chinese Language has separate rules; listed medium-of-instruction exemptions apply.
На английском
IELTS Academic 6.0 / TOEFL iBT 80 or recognized alternative/medium-of-instruction evidence; specified country exemptions.
Образование
High-school graduate or final-year student.
Возраст
Generally 18–30 by 1 September 2027; minors require the stated guarantee documentation.
CSCA
Required; subjects depend on discipline and teaching language.
Срок подачи
Opens 1 December 2026. Deadlines: CSC B 20 February 2027; provincial/university scholarships 30 April; self-funded 31 May.
Сбор за заявку
400 CNY
Стоимость обучения
Chinese-medium: 24,000 humanities / 26,000 other listed fields; English MBBS 38,000; English design/art 90,000 CNY/year.

Магистратура · 2027 intake

Проверено 2026-10-16

Версия источника Дата проверки не означает, что приём открыт. Уточните программу, набор и срок на официальном сайте.

На китайском
HSK 5 ≥210 humanities/economics/management/law/arts; HSK 4 ≥210 science/engineering/medicine/PE; previous Chinese-medium degree evidence accepted.
На английском
IELTS Academic 6.0 / TOEFL iBT 80 or recognized alternative; listed country/English-medium exemptions.
Образование
Bachelor's degree or final-year undergraduate.
Возраст
Generally under 45 by 1 September 2027; scholarship age limits can be lower.
CSCA
Не записано
Срок подачи
Opens 1 December 2026; CSC 20 February 2027, listed provincial/university scholarships 30 April, self-funded 31 May. CSC A uses separate dates.
Сбор за заявку
400 CNY
Стоимость обучения
Regular Chinese-medium 26,000–30,000 CNY/year; English-medium 39,000–45,000. Cooperative Design 120,000 and Drama/Film/Television 172,000 per year.
Другие требования
  • Two academic references; ≥1,000-word personal statement. Pre-acceptance is optional, with selection priority.
  • Regular English programs last 2 years; most Chinese programs 3, with named exceptions.

National programsИсточник

  • Project 985

    Project 985 was a national program (from 1998) to build world-class universities. The term is still widely used as a mark of the top tier.

  • Project 211

    Project 211 was a national program (from 1995) supporting more than 100 key universities.

  • Double First-Class

    Double First-Class is the current national program (from 2017) for building world-class universities and disciplines. This university is on the second-round list published in 2022.
